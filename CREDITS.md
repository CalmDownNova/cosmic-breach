Cosmic Breach is a NeoForge 1.21.1 mod by Nate (CalmDownNova), built with Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code.
This file ships inside the mod jar and at the root of the source repository.

## Audio: ElevenLabs

Some of the audio in this mod was made with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io). Every ElevenLabs take in this release
was generated under a paid ElevenLabs plan (Starter), which grants a commercial licence for the output. It is credited
here, covered by ElevenLabs' terms, and excluded from the MIT grant.

| What | ElevenLabs model | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Voice lines (text to speech, ElevenLabs premade voices) | ElevenLabs v4 (`eleven_v4`) | 19 lines, 3 voices |
| ElevenLabs sound effects (source takes) | `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` | 8 prompts, 10 takes |

Every take was fetched once and kept as a source file under `tools/sound/eleven/`, then filtered, layered and mastered
by the mod's own sound build. The build never calls the network.

Voices by ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io).

### One file derived from Minecraft

`assets/minecraft/shaders/include/fog.glsl` is Minecraft 1.21.1's own `fog.glsl` with extra fog shapes added. The
original is Mojang's; the modification is shipped the way resource packs and mods routinely override vanilla shaders.
It remains under Mojang's terms and is excluded from the MIT grant.

## Build tooling (source repository only, not in the jar)

NeoForge and ModDevGradle (NeoForged, LGPL-2.1), Parchment mappings (ParchmentMC), the Gradle wrapper (Apache-2.0),
JUnit 5 (EPL-2.0), and for the asset scripts Python with numpy, scipy and Pillow. Better Combat, Combat Roll and Cloth
Config appear in the build only as optional mods for a side-by-side comparison in the development client; they are
not dependencies and are not shipped.
