# Attunement

As you live in Aetheria, the song learns you. That growth is **Attunement**: its own experience, separate from
the kind you spend on enchanting. You gain it by defeating creatures, finding structures, solving their puzzles,
and above all by beating the guardians. Everyone who shares a fight gets the full share; nothing is split. Death
costs none of it.

<AttunementStatus />

Each level gives you **a point to spend**, and each guardian's first defeat gives one more. Spend them in the
allocation screen: press <KeyBind id="key.cosmicbreach.attunement" />, or <AllocationLink>open it from here</AllocationLink>.

The levels climb to fifty. What the four kinds of point do is on the [next page](attributes.md).
