# Activate and fall up

Hold a **Starfall Shard** and use it on any frame of a finished ring. The shard is spent, the frames
light, and the middle of the ring is no longer ground. It is sky, a window two blocks wide: look down
into it and you will see clouds drifting under your feet.

That is the **Breach**. Step into it.

The pull comes at once. You will rise, the world will turn white, and then you will be falling
through open air above the islands, slowly, with the wind holding you.

Nothing up there will trouble you for your first minute, unless you trouble it first.

If any frame is ever broken, its Breach closes. Another shard opens it again.
