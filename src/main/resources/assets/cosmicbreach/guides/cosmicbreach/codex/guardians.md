# Guardians and their lairs

Each layer of Aetheria has a guardian, and each guardian holds a key. Beat one and the song opens the way to the
layer below it: the Shear lets you through, and falls gently.

**Finding a lair.** The lairs are rare and far apart, but this book knows where they are. **Sneak and use the
Codex anywhere in Aetheria**, and it throws a mote of light toward the nearest lair of a guardian you have not
yet beaten, like an eye that remembers its home. Follow it. When no guardian is left, it points you to the
heart of the Breach.

**Finding a puzzle room.** Use the Codex again while its mote is still flying, and it turns to the nearest puzzle
room of the layer you stand in whose vault you have not yet opened. It keeps pointing there each time you use it,
until you turn it back the same way. The words under your hotbar always say what it is tracking. When no puzzle
room is left near you on this layer, it says so and points at the guardian again.

**Waking them.** A guardian sleeps as a statue until someone who has not yet beaten it comes near, or until
someone wakes it on purpose with a **Guardian Echo** (forged at the Astral Forge) used on its altar. A lair
rests for twenty minutes after each fight.

**Sharing the spoils.** Everyone who fights beside you, or simply stands in the arena, takes their own full
reward: their own Attunement, their own drops, their own attunement to the next layer. Helping a friend never
costs you anything.

A guardian's own page stays sealed until you have stood before it.
