Our ceremonial armor, ivory and gold, for **Arcane** and **Resilience**. Forged at the third tier from Eclipsium,
gold and Umbral Silk.

- **Two pieces:** out of combat your Resonance settles at half, not a third, and every ability costs a little
  less.
- **Four pieces, Harmonics:** every third ability you call within a short while **echoes** a moment later, at a
  little over half its strength, and costs you nothing. The halo spins faster when an echo waits.
- **Set ability, Hymn of Alignment:** a ring of light on the ground. Allies inside it cast faster and gather
  Resonance twice as fast; foes inside it are laid open to abilities.

The Regalia's glyphs pulse on Vesper's beat. They always have.
