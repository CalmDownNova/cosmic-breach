# Strikes and chains

- **Attack** (<KeyBind id="key.attack" />) strikes, and strikes again: each weapon has a **chain** of blows.
  Press again as one ends and the next follows; wait too long and the chain begins again. A press made in the
  last moments of a blow is remembered and fires as soon as it can.
- **Hold attack** to charge. You slow to a walk while the weapon gathers; let go to release a heavy blow.
- **Attack in the air** for an aerial strike. **Look steeply down** as you attack and it becomes a **plunge**:
  you drop like a stone, the blow grows with the height you fell, and the fall itself cannot hurt you.
- **Attack as a dash ends** for a dash strike, a blow that carries your momentum through the enemy.
- **Use** (<KeyBind id="key.use" />) calls your weapon's **ability**, its own verb. Abilities spend Resonance
  and rest a while after.

**Nothing cancels a blow once it has begun to gather.** That is the commitment. A dash can cut a blow's recovery
short, and an ability even sooner.

Every blow carries **Impact**. Enough Impact in a short time staggers a foe, and the great ones **Break**: their
guard falls open for a few precious seconds.
