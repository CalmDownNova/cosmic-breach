# Build a ring

A Breach Ring is twelve **Breach Frames** laid flat on the ground round the edge of a 4 by 4
square, with its middle, 2 by 2, left empty. Each frame is copper and stone: copper ingots in the
corners of the crafting grid, cobblestone or cobbled deepslate in the rest.

<RecipeFor id="cosmicbreach:breach_frame" />

One craft makes six frames, so two crafts make the ring: **8 Copper Ingots and 10 stone** in all.
Copper is the easy metal: look for its green-stained ore in any cave or ravine, and smelt it.
