# The Drift Manta

A pale manta the size of a boat, swimming through the air of the Drift and singing as it goes.

**Taming, by answering.** A manta sings three notes on Vesper's beat. Answer each with a **Resonance Chime**
(Forge II): each use plays the next note, and each must land on the beat. Three clean phrases in a row and it
stays with you. Miss, and it swims away for a while. Hold the Chime and a wild manta nearby comes to the edge of
your rock, within reach; once it is yours it swims to your side.

**Riding.** Harness it with a **Drift Harness** (Forge II). Inside the Drift it truly flies, and so it does in the
Deep: jump or look up to climb, look down to dive. In the Deep it climbs only as high as the dark reaches: a little
under the lower Shear band, where the climb eases off and stops. The manta will not carry you up through the band into
the Drift; the way up from the Deep is still the rising current. On the Upper Reach it can only glide. The dash key is
its **Phase Blink**: a leap forward through space, untouchable for a moment, rider and all.

**Gear:** **Nebulite Barding**, **Nebula Reins** (a quicker Phase Blink) and **Gale Fins** (faster flight).

**Keeping it safe.** A **Stable Crystal** (Forge I, in the Mount Gear tab) keeps a tamed manta, harness and all:
use it on the manta to stow it away, and on the ground to set it down beside you. A tamed manta shrugs off the
weather, your own blows, and any blow from a player who may not hurt you (all of them, where PvP is off). A parked
one stays where you leave it through a Gravity Tide, and one that falls below the Drift on its own climbs back into it (a manta you rode down into the Deep and left there stays where you left it). If the
Shear throws you back up, your manta comes with you. A crystal that holds a mount does not burn or vanish. If it falls
out of the world it goes straight to you, or waits for you if you are dead or away, and is in your pack when you
respawn or log in.
