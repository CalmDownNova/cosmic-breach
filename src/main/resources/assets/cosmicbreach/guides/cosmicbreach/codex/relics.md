# Relics of the Heliarch

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:last_light" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:umbra_cantor" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:heliarchs_crown" scale="2" />
</Row>

**Last Light**, the solar glaive, answers to **Resilience** (A) and **Power** (B). Its chain is three long thrusts.
Its ability, **Dawnguard** (35 Resonance), is a stance: for a moment every blow that reaches you is parried,
for a little Resonance each, and every parry answers with **Daybreak**, a wide counter slash. Each parry also
stores a charge of **Sunlight**, up to three; a charged attack spends them all.

**Umbra Cantor**, the longbow, answers to **Agility** (A) and **Arcane** (C). A charged shot leaves a resonant note
where it lands. Three notes close together form a **chord**: the triangle between them silences lesser foes and
wounds them while it lasts. Its ability, **Cadence**, looses three quick shots.

**The Heliarch's Crown** is a trophy. Set it on a pedestal and a small sun circles it, lighting the room.
