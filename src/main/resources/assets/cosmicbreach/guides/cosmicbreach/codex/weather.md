# Cosmic weather

Aetheria's sky changes by itself, one event at a time in each layer. Every event warns you first.

**Solar Flare** (the Reach). Solenne swells, the sky whitens and a hum rises. For a minute and a half, anyone under
the open sky is **Scorched**. Stand under anything: a single block overhead is shade. The Flare feeds you too:
Resonance comes twice as fast, solar blows burn hotter, and Shardlings run faster. A Star Egg left out under a
Flare hatches at once.

**Meteor Shower** (the Reach and the Drift). Red streaks cross the sky. Then, near each of you in turn, a red
circle and a whistle: a meteor lands there a moment later. Each leaves a **Meteorite** block behind, worth breaking.

<Gate when="drift" sealed="none">

**Gravity Tide** (the Drift). The dust streams all swing to one heading. For a while your weight almost vanishes,
a current carries everything one way, arrows fly further, and falling cannot hurt you. Fight in the air, or ride
the current.

</Gate>

<Gate when="deep" sealed="none">

**Eclipse Surge** (the Deep). Vesper falls silent and the lichen dims. For two minutes the hollow things hit
harder, come more often and step further through the dark; but abilities cost less. Light your ground.

</Gate>
