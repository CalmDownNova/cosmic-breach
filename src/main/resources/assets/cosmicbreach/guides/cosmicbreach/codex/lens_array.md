# The Lens Array

Deep in the old spires and towers you will find round rooms with a grid of pedestals and crystals set in
the walls. When you first come near, the Sun Aperture in the ceiling opens and a beam of sunlight falls
onto the grid.

**Light every crystal with its own colour at the same time, and hold it.** The vault of the room opens.

- **Mirrors** turn the light by a right angle. Use one to turn it a step clockwise; use it while sneaking to
  turn it back. A mirror rimmed in gold is **loose**: strike it with a bare hand to lift it, and use it on
  any empty pedestal of the same room to set it down, turned as it was.
- **Splitter prisms** take light from their point and send it out to both sides.
- **Filter lenses** tint whatever passes through them: gold, teal or magenta.
- **Umbral blocks** drink the light. They cannot be lifted, but a heavy blow knocks one a tile along the
  grid: a charged strike, the Comet Maul, a plunge from above.
- **Receptor crystals** hum. The closer the light passes, the higher they sing.

Nothing can be built on the grid, and none of its pieces will break. Take your time: the only danger is
the **Warden Eye** in the wall. Send the light into it and it calls its hunters.
