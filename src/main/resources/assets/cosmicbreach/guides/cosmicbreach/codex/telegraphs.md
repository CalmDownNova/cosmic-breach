# Reading a foe

Every creature of Aetheria warns you before it strikes, in one language. Learn it once and it serves you
everywhere, from the smallest Shardling to the things that wait at the bottom of the world.

- **A gold glint** and a rising "ting": the blow can be **parried**.
- **A red glint** and a low horn: it cannot. **Move.**
- **A shape on the ground** shows where a blow will land. Its outline is the place; its fill grows toward the
  edge, and the blow lands when it is full. The mark is the clock.
- **A thin red line** is a path: something will travel along it. Leave the line.
- Any blow that could take a fifth of your health gives you more than half a second of warning. If you were hit
  by something you never saw coming, look again: it told you.

The great ones never shorten their warnings, however long a fight goes on. They grow harder by weaving their
attacks together, never by cheating.
