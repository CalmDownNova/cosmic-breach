# A sealed page

Somewhere in the Deep stands a hall that swallows sound. What was sung there once still waits to be finished, and
this page will stay silent until you have heard it.

Sneak and use this book anywhere in Aetheria to see which way to go.

</Gate>

# The Unsung

Three porcelain masks of the Choir, **Alto**, **Tenor** and **Bass**, singing one song that never ends. They share
one life.

**Only the singing mask can be hurt,** and the song passes from mask to mask every eight beats of Vesper. Watch for
the one that shines.

- **Alto** sends notes that follow you: dodge them.
- **Tenor** sweeps a wave of sound across the floor: jump it.
- **Bass** sends ripples through the ground that throw you back.

Every so often all three **harmonize**, and the whole hall rings. Only the **circles of silence** on the floor
shelter you from it: when the harmony gathers, find one.

Everything they do falls on the beat. Listen to Vesper and you will know when.

Their first defeat gives you a **Silent Sigil**, a **Choir Pendant**, a point to spend, and the right to enter the
Breach Sanctum.

</Gate>
