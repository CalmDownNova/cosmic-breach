# Starfall Vanguard

Meteor-forged plate, for **Power** and **Resilience**, forged at the first tier from Starsteel and Starshards. In
the Vanguard, falling becomes a weapon.

- **Two pieces, Heavy Landing:** falls hurt you half as much, and landing from four blocks or more sends out a
  shockwave that grows with the height.
- **Four pieces, Meteoric Momentum:** the fall you did not feel is kept as **Heat**, and your next blow spends
  it. The seams glow brighter the more Heat you carry. You stand steadier while charging.
- **Set ability, Meteor Call:** mark a spot. A burning circle shows everyone where; a moment later a meteor
  lands there and leaves the ground on fire.

Climb, then fall. The Vanguard was made for that.
