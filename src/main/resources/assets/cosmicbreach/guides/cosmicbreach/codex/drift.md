# The Drift

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:driftstone" scale="2" />

Below the Reach, below the first Shear, the world comes apart into stones: asteroids of grey-blue **Driftstone**
turning slowly in belts, with open lanes of sky between them. Thalassa hangs enormous overhead.

**Here your weight thins.** Everything falls at less than half its usual pace: you, your foes, your arrows, the
things you drop. You jump higher and fall further without harm. Inside a belt the stones crowd close: most lie a
running jump from the next, and small **stepping stones** hang across many of the wider gaps. Some gaps hold none:
one just past a running jump yields to a dash in the air, and a wider one, like the open lanes between the belts, is
for the Driftweave's Drift, or for a **Drift Manta**.

- **Nebulite Ore** hides in the hearts of the big asteroids. It needs an iron pickaxe, or the Reach's Starsteel
  one. Nebulite Ingots make a **Nebulite Pickaxe**, which mines whatever a diamond one does.
- Petrified **Driftwood** trees grow on the largest ones, and **Rimeglass** glitters in the cold places: crush it
  into **Rime Thread**, the Drift's string.
- Wild **Drift Mantas** give **Manta Fillets** (sear them) and **Starhide**. A tamed one gives nothing but its gear.
- **Drift currents** are the visible streams of dust: anything inside one is carried along.

A second Shear lies below the Drift. It will catch you as the first one did, until the song carries you
further.
