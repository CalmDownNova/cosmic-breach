# The Hollow Heliarch

Our regent. When the Hollow broke through beneath the forge, the Heliarch poured our sun into the crack to seal it,
and the Hollow took the light, and the regent with it. What is left is a ring of armored plates round a burning
core, and two great hands.

- **As Regent** it fights with the sun it remembers. A hand rises over a **gold** ring: parry it, and its guard
  cracks. A band of fire sweeps the floor between two rings of the arena: jump it, or stand inside or outside it.
  A thin red line tracks you and then turns white: step aside after it locks. Its plates fly out to the rim and
  come back through the middle.
- **Hollowed,** its plates tear free and stand in the arena as **monoliths**, and its core becomes an eclipse
  high above the pillars, out of reach. Its **tendrils** rise from cracks in the floor: every blow you land on a
  tendril reaches the Heliarch itself. When the eclipse's beam sweeps the arena, hide behind a monolith; each beam
  wears one down.
- **Nova.** Wounded enough, it gathers itself behind a shield of light. Break the shield before the light is
  full and it falls stunned, its heart exposed. Fail, and only a monolith will save you.
- **Collapse.** At the very end the Breach widens under the arena and the floor falls away, piece by piece.

**The Breach forgives a fall.** Step off the edge and it throws you back onto the rim, weakened, and your
abilities will not answer for a moment.

It shares nothing: when it falls, each of you opens your own **Reliquary**.

</Gate>
