# Rings, pendants and charms

You have four more places to wear the Choir's work: **two rings**, **a necklace** and **a charm**. Open them with
<KeyBind id="key.curios.open.desc" />, or the small button in your inventory.

## Rings

- **Perihelion Loop** (a Reliquary's vault): sharper eyes for critical strikes, and critical strikes just after a
  dash hit harder.

<Gate when="drift" sealed="none">

- **Twin Comet Band** (an Observatory's vault): one more dash in the air, and dashes in the air chain into each
  other.
- **Gravity Loop** (sometimes carried by Gyre Knights): your plunges have no ceiling, and a long plunge opens a
  small Gravity Well where you land.

</Gate>

<Gate when="deep" sealed="none">

- **Leechstar Signet** (a Crypt's vault): a parry heals you, a little plus a share of what it stopped.

</Gate>

## Necklaces

<Gate when="met_colossus" sealed="Each necklace is a guardian's gift. None has been given to you yet.">

- **Heart of a Dying Star** (the Prism Colossus): more health, and when yours runs low a nova throws your foes
  back and hardens you for a moment. Then it rests a while.

</Gate>

<Gate when="met_unsung" sealed="none">

- **Choir Pendant** (the Unsung): more Resonance, and abilities that strike many foes pay you back for each.

</Gate>

## Charms

- **Sunshard Compass** (rarely, a Reliquary's vault): points to the nearest vault you have not opened, and shows
  hidden traps near you at any speed.

<Gate when="met_leviathan" sealed="none">

- **Halo of Nine** (the Thalassine Leviathan): three shards circle you, each stopping one arrow or bolt and
  regrowing after. With enough Arcane they cut what they pass through.

</Gate>

<Gate when="sanctum" sealed="none">

- **Event Horizon Lens** (the Sanctum's west wing): a wider parry, and a parry made early drops a small black hole
  on the attacker.
- **Hourglass of Vesper** (the Sanctum's east wing): a perfect dodge slows every foe near you for a heartbeat.

</Gate>
