# Comet Maul

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:comet_maul" scale="2" />

A greathammer with a comet for a head. Slow, heavy, and the best thing in Aetheria for breaking a guard. It
answers to **Power** (A) and **Resilience** (C), and it is forged at the second tier from Nebulite, a Heartstone,
Starshards and Driftwood.

**It does not flinch.** While a Maul blow gathers and lands, blows that would stagger you do not.

- **The chain:** an overhead smash, a side swing, then a full spin into a slam.
- **Charged, Impact Crater:** leap, and crack the ground; the crater slows whatever stands in it.
- **In the air, Meteorfall:** a head-first dive with a comet's tail.
- **Dashing, Ram:** a shoulder charge behind the haft.
- **Ability, Gravity Well** (40 Resonance): plant the Maul, and a well pulls every lesser foe toward it, then
  **Collapses** on them. You are rooted while it pulls, but a dash frees you, and fires the Collapse early.

The Maul's heavy blows can move things that no hand can lift. Remember that in the rooms of light.
