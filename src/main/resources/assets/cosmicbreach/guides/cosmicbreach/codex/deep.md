# The Deep

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:magenta_neon_lichen" scale="2" />

The bottom layer of Aetheria: black arches and needles of **Umbral Basalt** rising out of nothing, linked by
natural bridges over the void. Below them there is no ground at all, only the dark, and the dark does not give
back what falls into it.

**It is the only dark place in Aetheria,** and that is on purpose. The layers above shade it; the sun reaches it
as a memory. What light there is comes from the **Neon Lichen**, magenta and teal, and from what you bring.

- **Bring light.** Torches and lanterns make safe ground here. Some things of the Deep will not cross it.
- **Eclipsium Ore** is the Deep's metal. It needs a diamond pickaxe.
- **Rift Scars** are holes in the platforms: mind your step.

At the very middle of the Deep the Breach itself opens, a chasm three hundred blocks across, and something floats
in it, far out over the void, still faintly burning.
