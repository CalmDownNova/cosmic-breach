# Binary Edges

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:binary_edges" scale="2" />

Two sickles, like a binary star: fast strings, dashes woven through them, and a thrown blade you can follow. They
answer to **Agility** (A) and **Power** (D), and they are forged at the second tier from two Gyre Blades, Nebulite
and Rimeglass.

- **The chain:** five blows, from quick hooks to a spinning whirl.
- **Weave:** attack just after a dash and the chain starts at its fourth blow, the lunge. Dash, strike, dash,
  strike: the Edges never stand still.
- **Charged, Binary Orbit:** both sickles spin round you on their chains while you move.
- **Ability, Tether** (25 Resonance): throw the left sickle. It sticks in whatever it hits. Use again and you
  **blink** to it, untouchable on the way, and slash as you arrive. A foe the blade struck is **Marked**, and
  easier to strike true for a few seconds. While the blade is out you fight with the right one alone.
