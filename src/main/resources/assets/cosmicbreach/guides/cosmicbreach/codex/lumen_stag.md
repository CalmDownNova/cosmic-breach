# The Lumen Stag

A tall white stag with crystal antlers that hold light. Herds graze the **Sunfield Terraces**.

**Taming, by trust.** A herd bolts from anyone who runs near it. Come **sneaking**, and hand a **Starbloom** to a
stag from right beside it. Each one earns a little trust, and its antlers glow brighter with each; when they
blaze, it is yours. Run near the herd, or strike any of them, and all of them trust you less.

**Riding.** Saddle it with an **Astral Saddle** (Forge I). A stag leaps three times, twice in the air; hold jump
as you fall and it glides far. The dash key makes its next leap. From its back you may still strike with the first
two blows of your chain, and call your weapon's ability.

**Gear**, forged: Starsteel or Nebulite **barding** for armor, a **Comet Bridle** for a fourth leap, **Halo
Reins** for a longer glide. The stag's screen has a slot for the tack.

**Keeping it safe.** A **Stable Crystal** (Forge I, in the Mount Gear tab) keeps one tamed mount, gear and all.
Use it on your stag to stow it away, and on solid ground to set it down beside you. A tamed stag shrugs off the
weather, your own blows, and any blow from a player who may not hurt you (all of them, where PvP is off). If it
falls below the islands it climbs back to where it last stood. A crystal that holds a mount does not burn or
vanish. If it falls out of the world it goes straight to you, or waits for you if you are dead or away, and is in
your pack when you respawn or log in.
