# The Starfall

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:starfall_shard" scale="2" />

At your first sunset, watch the sky. A star will cross it, burning white and gold, and come down
not far from you. You will hear it land. Then a pillar of light will stand where it fell, for about
five minutes. Follow it.

In a small crater you will find the **Starfall Shard**, still glowing. Your bare hand is enough to
take it.

One falls for you on every night after that, at some hour between dusk and midnight. Sleep through
the night and it will be waiting at dawn.

Some fell long ago. Their craters are wide and old, and they are called **Fallen Rifts**: a broken
ring lies half buried in each, eight of its twelve frames still in place, with a chest beside it. A traveller who finds one early may be above
the clouds before the first night.
