# Shardlings

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:starshard" scale="2" />

Crystal hunters the size of a fox, all spines and facets. They hunt in packs of three to five, and they fight
like one animal.

- One **taunts**: it holds in front of you and feints.
- The others **flank**: they circle behind you, and wait. When one lands a hit, it takes the taunter's place.
- Only two may strike at you at once. A pack is pressure, never a swarm.

**Read the spines.** Before a **Splinter Lunge** a Shardling crouches and its back spines flash **gold**, with a
rising "ting": that lunge can be parried. Beyond six blocks it spits three needles instead; its throat glows
first.

When one dies it bursts into three chiming shards that explode a moment later, so kill one in the middle of the
pack and the fight moves. They love light: under a Solar Flare they run faster, and the shade of a spire is
your friend.

They drop **Starshards**, sometimes a **Starsteel Nugget**, rarely **Starbloom Seeds**.
