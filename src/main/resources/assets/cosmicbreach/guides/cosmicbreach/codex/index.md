# The Starfall Codex

You found this book the way we all did: it fell.

Above your sky there is another one. We call it Aetheria. Islands of white stone drift there in sunlight that
never quite ends, and under them the air goes down and down, through belts of turning stone, into a deep that
glows.

The way up is not hidden. The sky itself hands it to you.
