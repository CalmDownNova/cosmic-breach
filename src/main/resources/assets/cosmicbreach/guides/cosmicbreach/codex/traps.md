# Traps of the Deep

- **Void Rift tiles.** Hairline cracks with a violet shimmer. Stand on them and after a moment a patch of floor
  simply vanishes. The fall ends in a **Void Pocket**, a sealed room with Stalkers waiting; the way out opens when
  they are dead, or after a while. Dashing across is safe.
- **Crushing Gravity Plates.** Faint rings on the floor, a hum from the ceiling. Step on the sigil and your weight
  triples: no jumping, slow feet, short dashes, and a moment later the piston above slams down. Get off the sigil
  before it does.
- **Kinetic Tripwires.** Near-invisible threads. Walking through is safe; anything faster fires bolts from the
  walls.
- **Starfall Chutes.** Openings in the ceiling that drop star-rocks in patterns. Each glints before it drops:
  learn the rhythm and walk through the gaps.

A **Sunshard Compass** shows every tell near you, however fast you move.
