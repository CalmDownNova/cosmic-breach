# Resonance

Resonance is the song in you, and your abilities are paid in it.

- **Fighting fills it.** Every hit you land adds some; a perfect dodge adds more, a parry most of all.
- **Waiting never fills it.** Out of combat it drifts slowly toward a third of its fullness, from above or below.
- Your **Arcane** raises how much you can hold.

You do not need a bar to know it. Your weapon glows brighter as Resonance fills, and when it is full it throws
sparks and hums on Vesper's beat. A thin arc round your crosshair shows it too, and your dashes as small pips;
both can be switched off in the mod's settings.

Vesper, the small blue star that pulses over every sky of Aetheria, beats a hundred times a minute. Much of this
world keeps its time: the music, the lichen, the old Choir's puzzles. Learn to hear it.
