# A sealed page

Somewhere among the stones of the Drift, a great ring of asteroids turns round an empty heart, and something
sings inside it. You will hear it long before you see it. This page waits for that meeting.

Sneak and use this book anywhere in Aetheria to see which way to go.

</Gate>

# The Thalassine Leviathan

A sky whale, long as a river, circling the inside of its Rift. Stand on the stones at the Rift's edge and strike it
as it passes; its song tells you what it will do.

- **Breach Dive.** Its song swells and a glowing **wake** marks the path it is about to take, with a ring where it
  will land. Be anywhere else.
- **Song of Pulling.** It drags you toward its mouth. Dash away from it, and keep dashing.
- **Moorage.** Wounded, it coils round the great stone at the Rift's heart and holds still for a while. Glowing
  **song glands** open along its back: leap onto it and strike them, for they take twice the harm. It moors more
  than once, but not forever.

Its first defeat gives you a **Leviathan Pearl** (which raises the Astral Forge to its third tier), a **Halo of
Nine**, **Leviathan Scales**, a point to spend, and the song that carries you down into the Deep.

</Gate>
