# Star Eggs and familiars

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:star_egg" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:familiar_lantern" scale="2" />
</Row>

**Star Eggs** turn up in vaults. Set one in a **Brazier of Solenne** (Forge I) and keep it warm for ten minutes,
or leave it under the open sky during a **Solar Flare** and it hatches at once. What hatches binds itself to a
**Familiar Lantern**, and the lantern *is* the familiar: nothing to lose, nothing to leave behind.

- **Use the lantern**, or **hold** <KeyBind id="key.cosmicbreach.familiar" />, to call or dismiss it.
- **Tap** <KeyBind id="key.cosmicbreach.familiar" /> to switch between **Attack**, **Guard** and **Passive**.
- One familiar at a time. It grows with you (your Resilience and Arcane). If it falls, the lantern goes dark for a
  minute. Familiars fly, never spring traps, and come back to you if you leave them behind.

There are three:

- **Emberwisp**, a small sun sprite: now and then it sets **Scorch** on a foe you struck; your perfect dodges and
  parries leave you **Kindled**, striking harder for a moment.
- **Gravikin**, a pebble golem: it **taunts** nearby foes onto itself, and slows whatever stands next to it.
- **Prism Moth**, all glass wings: it lays **Refract** on your target, up to three; your next ability spends
  them for a stronger blow. It also clears one ill from you now and then.
