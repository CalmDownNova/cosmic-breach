# Choir Astrolabe

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:choir_astrolabe" scale="2" />

A handheld astrolabe whose rings spin as you fight. It strikes from afar with small stars that bend a little
toward their mark, and every star is a note: a chain of them is a melody. It answers to **Arcane** (S) and
**Agility** (D), and it is forged at the third tier from a Leviathan Scale, Eclipsium, Spire Quartz and Umbral
Silk.

- **The chain:** two star bolts, then a triad of three.
- **Charged, Constellation:** hold, and sweep your aim across up to five foes; release to link them all with one
  beam.
- **In the air:** a bolt fired down that bursts where it lands. **Dashing:** a decoy star that fires on its own.
- **Ability, Pocket Star** (50 Resonance): set a small sun where you aim. It pulses, swallows arrows and bolts
  that come near it, and lights the ground so brightly that certain things of the dark will not cross it. Use
  again for a **Supernova**: the sooner you call it, the harder it bursts.

A Pocket Star inside a Comet Maul's Gravity Well pulls twice as hard, and bursts half again as hard. We called it
a **Singularity**.
