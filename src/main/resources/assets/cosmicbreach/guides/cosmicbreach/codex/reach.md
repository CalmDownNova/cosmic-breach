# The Upper Reach

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:glimmer_grass" scale="2" />

The highest layer of Aetheria: islands of **Starfall Stone** floating in open sky, crowned with **Spire Quartz**
and carpeted in **Glimmer Grass**. The sun here is Solenne, three times the size of yours, and night is only
the hours when the great ringed planet Thalassa passes in front of her.

- The **Shattered Spires** cover most of the Reach: tall crystal spires, some leaning, some snapped, their fallen
  tops bridging the gaps between islands. **Starsteel Ore** hides in their cores, often laid bare where a spire
  broke. A stone pickaxe is enough to mine it.
- The **Sunfield Terraces** are the quiet quarter: broad golden steps and shallow pools, where the Lumen Stags
  graze and nothing hunts. It is the best place in Aetheria to build a home.

**Everything you need is here.** Fallen **Driftwood** lies on the ground of many islands: break it by hand for
planks, sticks, a crafting table and fuel. Where none lies, **Halo Moss** will do: cut a strand from below an island
and craft it into planks. **Starfall Stone** makes stone tools and a furnace. Halo Moss also drops a **Halo Berry**
now and then, unless shears take the strand whole, and a wild Lumen Stag gives **Lumen Venison** (sear it in a
furnace) and **Starhide**, the Reach's leather. A stag you have tamed gives nothing but its gear. Smelt Raw Starsteel into ingots and craft a
**Starsteel Pickaxe**: it mines whatever an iron one does.

**Falling here teaches instead of killing.** Far below the islands lies a band of empty sky, the first **Shear**.
Until the song will carry you further, it catches you and throws you back up to where you last stood, for a
little of your health. Beds work during the eclipse, and you may set your spawn here.

Look over an island's edge and you will see what waits below: stones turning in the haze, and further down, a
faint glow. You cannot go there yet.
