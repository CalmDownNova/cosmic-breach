# The Gyre Observatory

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:observatory_vault" scale="2" />

Our watchtowers, grown right through asteroids of the Drift: five to eight floors, and no stairs between them.
Instead, columns of rising air, the **gravity lifts**, carry you up; step out of one to stop.

The Observatories are guarded:

- **Kinetic Tripwires** are threads you can barely see, strung across the halls. **Walk** through them and nothing
  happens. Cross one faster than a walk, sprinting or dashing, and the emitters in the walls fire bolts along
  it, harder the faster you went. Moving carefully shows you where they are.
- A **Gyre Knight** keeps the telescope chamber at the top.

That chamber holds a **Lens Array** seven pedestals wide, with colored filters: some crystals want gold light, some
teal, some magenta. Its **vault** holds Drift treasures, and sometimes a ring.

**Finding one.** Sneak and use the Codex, then use it again while its mote flies: it turns to the nearest puzzle
room on your layer whose vault you have not opened yet.
