# Driftweave

Light plates over star-dust cloth, cut for the Drift, for **Agility** and **Power**. Forged at the second tier
from Nebulite, Rimeglass and string (the coat wants a Gyre Core).

- **Two pieces:** one more dash, and every dash carries you further.
- **Four pieces, Slipstream:** a perfect dodge leaves an **Afterimage** where you stood. For a few seconds it
  repeats your next blows from there, more faintly.
- **Set ability, Drift:** for a few seconds your own weight thins as it does in the Drift. You jump higher, dashes
  in the air cost nothing, and blows struck from the air land harder.

The scarf shows your ready dashes: one band lit for each.
