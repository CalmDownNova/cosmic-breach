# The Breach Sanctum

At the bottom of the world, where the Breach opens onto nothing, the Choir's last hall still floats over the dark.
Its ivory has gone grey and its gold has tarnished, but the last embers of Solenne burn in it, and from any
platform of the Deep you can see their glow hanging over the chasm. A causeway of old stone runs out to it from the
edge.

**The Gate** opens for anyone the Unsung has attuned to the Sanctum. Whoever stands within sixteen blocks of them
when it opens comes in as their party, and the Gate remembers them: they may come and go as they please. To everyone
else it stays shut.

**The way back up** is the way you came: the Gate opens for you from inside, and the causeway leads out to the
platform of the Deep.

**The Antechamber** divides in two.

- **West, the Lens of Solenne:** the greatest of the Lens Arrays, seven by seven, three crystals to light, with
  splitters, filters and Umbral blocks in the way.
- **East, the Choir of the Unsung:** the hardest Choir Floor, three rounds, the ring turning, a ghost note.

Each wing's vault opens once for each of you. And each wing's answer lights one of the two **Eclipse Locks** beside
the sealed stair at the far end of the hall. When both burn, the **Throne Stair** opens and leads down to the
Sanctum floor: a great disc over the Breach, its throne at the middle, eight Choir Pillars round it.

**The floor forgives a fall.** Step off its edge and the Breach throws you back onto the rim, but it takes half your
strength and leaves you **Voidsick** for a few moments: your weapon's power won't answer until it passes.

**The Dying Star Heart** is forged at the Astral Forge's third tier from the Unsung's Silent Sigil, the Solar Ember
of the west wing, the Hymn Crystal of the east wing and four Eclipsium Ingots. It belongs on the throne. If what
answers is not defeated, the Heart comes back to whoever set it there, so you can try again.
