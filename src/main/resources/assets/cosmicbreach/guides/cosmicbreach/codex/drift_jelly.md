# The Drift Jelly

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:drift_gel" scale="2" />

A bell three blocks wide, deep violet over a body like dark glass, with a lit core inside and magenta and teal
markings. Long tendrils hang below it. It drifts through the air of the Deep without a care and bobs where it hangs.

- **It is not hostile.** Left alone it just drifts, a few blocks over the ground in the Lichen Gardens and the Spans,
  and now and then out over the gaps, where it makes a stepping stone. Hit it and it slowly floats away.
- **The tendrils sting.** Anything that stands in the tendrils under the bell takes a small sting about once a second,
  and is not pushed by it. Go round, or hit the bell from the side.
- **Land on the bell.** It is solid on top and it bounces. A landing sends you higher than a slime block would, and
  the harder you fall the higher you go. A gentle landing still gives you a good hop. Sneak to rest on it instead.
  Every bounce gives you **Skim** for a few seconds: you steer 30 percent better in the air. Over a void, a jelly
  is a way across, and a good way to reach the Hanging Wood and the Shattered Field.
- **Blooms.** Rarely, a whole swarm of jellies, fifteen to twenty five, rises from far below the layer, drifts up
  through it for a few minutes and then sinks away again. While it lasts, the sky of the Deep is full of
  stepping stones.

It drops **Drift Gel**. Eaten raw it feeds a little and gives you night vision for half a minute, which is a lot in
the Deep. Cooked on a **campfire**, in a **furnace** or in a **smoker** it becomes **Candied Gel**, a much better
snack, though the glow is cooked out of it.
