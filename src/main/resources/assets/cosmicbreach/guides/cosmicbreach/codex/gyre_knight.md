# The Gyre Knight

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:gyre_core" scale="2" />

An empty suit of silver-blue armor round a spinning gyroscope, three blades orbiting it on tilted rings. It flies,
and at first it uses the Drift far better than you do. Its rings always tell you what comes next: listen to the
pitch of its hum, and watch how wide they spread.

- **Shield Orbit:** rings tight, a low steady hum. The blades guard its front against anything thrown. Get behind
  it.
- **Sweep Orbit:** the rings swell with a rising whine, and the blades glint **gold**. Parry a blade and it
  breaks for a few seconds.
- **Lance Volley:** a **red** line reaches from each blade to you. They fire one after another: dash across the
  lines. While its blades are away, its core lies open.
- **Recall Crash:** once it is badly hurt, red lines run from each blade back to its core, through where you
  stand. Leave the lines.

Break its guard and it drops to the nearest ground, stunned, its core open.

It carries a **Gyre Core**, Nebulite, often a **Gyre Blade** (the Binary Edges want two), and rarely a
**Gravity Loop**.
