# The Hollow Stalker

<ItemImage id="cosmicbreach:mask_shard" scale="2" />

A tall, thin shape of void wearing a porcelain mask with magenta eyes. It lives in the dark and hates light.

- **It comes through the dark.** It will go a long way round to stay in shadow, and it will never step into
  strong light. A torch is a wall to it.
- **It prefers your back.** When you are not looking, it can step from one dark place to another in an instant.
- **Look at it.** Caught in your gaze up close, it freezes, and its mask flares. Paying attention is rewarded.
- **Rend:** its mask flares magenta, a whisper rises, and at the last moment a **gold** glint: parry it. It leaves
  a mark of **Rift** on you, which weakens your armor for a while.
- **Grasp:** if you stand in the dark with your back to it, a quiet whisper behind you is all the warning you get.
  It holds you and drains you until you dash or parry free.

Every ability you call leaves light where it strikes for a moment. The Astrolabe's Pocket Star lights the ground so
fiercely that a Stalker will not come near it.

It leaves **Umbral Silk**, sometimes an **Eclipsium Nugget**, and very rarely a shard of its mask.
