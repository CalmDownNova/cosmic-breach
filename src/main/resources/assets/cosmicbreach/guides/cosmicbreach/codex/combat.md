# Combat

We fought with the song, and so will you. The weapons of the Choir do not simply swing: every blow is a
commitment. It gathers, it strikes, it recovers, and while it recovers you are open. Reading an enemy and
choosing between a strike, a dash and a parry is the whole art.

While a weapon of the Choir is in your hand, it takes over your attack and use keys. Put it away and everything
works as it always has.
