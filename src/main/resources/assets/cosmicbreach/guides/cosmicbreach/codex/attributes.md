# Power, Agility, Arcane, Resilience

- **Power** makes your blows hit harder, as far as your weapon's grade in Power allows, and your critical strikes
  hit harder still.
- **Agility** sharpens your eye for critical strikes, brings your dashes back sooner, lengthens the moment a
  dash protects you, and at twenty grants a third dash. It never makes a swing faster: every blow keeps its
  timing, so what you learn stays true.
- **Arcane** deepens your Resonance, shortens every rest your abilities take, and strengthens the abilities
  themselves.
- **Resilience** adds to your health, takes the edge off every blow, steadies you against staggers, and widens
  your parry.

Each weapon lists the virtues it answers to, from **S** (fully) down to **D** (a little). Each point gives a
little less than the one before, so no single number runs away.

You may put at most **thirty** points in one virtue; armor and relics can raise it past that.

**Changed your mind?** A **Reverie Draught** returns every point you have spent. The Astral Forge makes them, and
the song gives you your first one at level ten.
