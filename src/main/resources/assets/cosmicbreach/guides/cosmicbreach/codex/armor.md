# Armor

The Choir made three kinds of armor, one for each layer, each built round two virtues and a verb of its own.
Wear two pieces of a set and it begins to answer; wear all four and it sings. A full set also grants its
**set ability**, called with <KeyBind id="key.cosmicbreach.set_ability" />, which rests a while after each use.

The armor is part of how you read yourself: its seams, cloth and halos change with what you carry and what you
are ready to do.
