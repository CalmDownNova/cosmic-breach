# Reforging

A weapon or piece of armor you love can follow you all the way down. Put it in the Forge's **reforge slot** and
it rises one tier: stronger by about a sixth, with a new trim and a new glow in the tier's colour.

- To the second tier: four **Nebulite Ingots**.
- To the third: four **Eclipsium Ingots**.
- To the fourth: a **Solar Heart**.

A Forge can only reforge up to its own tier, and a piece can climb no higher than the fourth. The colour of a
piece's outline in your inventory tells its tier at a glance: gold, cyan, violet, then white gold.

Reforging is the road to more damage. The Choir's weapons take no enchantments and never wear out: what they are
is what the Forge makes of them.
