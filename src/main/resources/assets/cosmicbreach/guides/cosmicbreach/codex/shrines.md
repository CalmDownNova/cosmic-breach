# Shrines

Near every guardian's lair stands a shrine, shaped by the guardian it watches over. Use one
(<KeyBind id="key.use" />) and it keeps your place: after a fall you wake beside it.

While a shrine keeps your place, dying anywhere in Aetheria costs you nothing. Everything you carry, everything you
wear and all your experience come back with you. Sleeping in a bed, or using another shrine, moves your place there.

A lair's shrine stands where the way on begins: beside its door, or at the edge above the drop to the layer below.

**The way back up.** Where a shrine stands at the edge of a drop, a golden current rises beside it, from the layer
below up through the Shear. You can see it from far off and hear it from close by. Walk or jump into it and it carries
you up and sets you down beside the shrine. Sneak and it lets you go. It never catches a fall: drop in from above
and you land where you land.
