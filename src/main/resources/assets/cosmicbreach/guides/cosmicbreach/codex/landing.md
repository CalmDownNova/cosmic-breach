# The Landing and the way home

Where you come down, a **Landing** will be waiting: a square of Starfall Stone bricks on the island
beneath you, six blocks across, with a ring already lit in its middle. Travellers who arrive near an old Landing share
it; no creature is born close to one.

The ring on the Landing is your way home. Step into it, and you will fall back into your own sky and
land beside the ring you left from. If that ring is gone, you land where you stand, on the ground
below; if you never had one, at the world's spawn.

The frames of a Landing's ring do not break. The way home stays open.
