# Dash and parry

**Dash** (<KeyBind id="key.cosmicbreach.dash" />) throws you a few blocks toward where you are moving, or back if
you are standing still. For its first moments nothing can touch you. You carry two dashes; they show as small
motes circling your feet, and each comes back after a breath.

A hit that lands in the very first instant of a dash is a **perfect dodge**: you gain Resonance, the dash comes
straight back, and your next blow is a certain critical strike.

**Parry** (<KeyBind id="key.cosmicbreach.parry" />) raises your guard for a heartbeat. Catch a blow in it and the
blow is gone: you gain a great deal of Resonance, the attacker reels, and your next hit is a **Riposte** that
strikes half again as hard. Miss, and you stand open for a moment.

**Only gold can be parried.** When an enemy's attack flashes **gold**, with a rising "ting", your guard can take
it. When it flashes **red**, with a low horn, it cannot: dash, jump, or be somewhere else.

Dashing, plunging and the drifting weight of the lower layers are weapons too. A fighter who stands still is
a fighter who has already chosen to be hit.
